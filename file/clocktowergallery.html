The Story

Brief History

The Marikina Clock Tower is located inside Marikina Cityhood Park at the corner of Sumulong Highway and Shoe Avenue. It was unveiled in 2005 to mark an anniversary of Marikina's cityhood. The monument stands 12 meters tall and is topped with a clock and 12 bells cast in Italy. Its Spanish-inspired facade and fountain at the base make it a recognizable city landmark.
